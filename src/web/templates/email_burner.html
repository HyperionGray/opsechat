⚠️ MVP scope: burner mailboxes are receive-focused aliases only. They exist to give you a short-lived address and a hidden read key. Senders can post plain-text messages through HTTP, but this page is optimized for receiving and reading, not for acting like a general outbound email client.
How this works:
  • Each burner email maps to a hidden HTTP mailbox plus a private read key.
  • The burner email is what you share; the read key stays private.
  • Messages remain memory-only and expire automatically.
  • No attachments, HTML mail, or arbitrary hosting payloads are supported.

Operational notes

  • These burner aliases are for inbound receipt in the MVP, not a full email ecosystem.
  • Senders should use the HTTP mail flow and the mailbox address behind the burner alias.
  • All data stays in memory and disappears on restart or expiry.
  • Use PGP or other client-side encryption if the message contents are sensitive.
Privacy notes:
  • Burner aliases expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • All mailbox material is lost on server restart.
  • Read keys are sensitive; anyone holding one can open the inbox.
  • Access through Tor remains the recommended posture for hostile environments.
