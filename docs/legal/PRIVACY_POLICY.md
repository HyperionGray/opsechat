## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information opsechat may process, how that
information is used, and your choices when using the service.

The service is designed to minimize data retention. Most content is ephemeral
and stored in memory only.

---

## 2. Data Minimization by Design

We intentionally limit persistent data collection:

- In-memory processing where possible
- Automatic expiration for ephemeral chat and DM content
- No long-term chat history by default
- No sale of personal data

---

## 3. Information We May Process

Depending on feature usage, we may process:

- Session identifiers
- Message metadata (timestamps, room/message identifiers)
- Temporary in-memory message content
- Technical logs for reliability and abuse prevention

For encrypted features, we do not have access to plaintext private keys.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate and secure the service
- Enforce abuse-prevention controls (rate limiting, anti-spam checks)
- Diagnose reliability issues
- Comply with legal obligations

---

## 5. Retention

Retention is intentionally short for many features:

- Ephemeral chat messages: approximately 3 minutes
- Direct messages used for room sharing: approximately 1 minute
- Other in-memory artifacts: removed as sessions expire or processes restart

Because this platform is privacy-oriented, retention periods may be adjusted to
improve safety, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

---

## 6. Security Practices

We apply security controls such as:

- Input sanitization and security headers
- Session-based rate limiting
- Memory cleanup for expired ephemeral records
- Limited service metadata exposure

No system can guarantee perfect security. Use the service with appropriate
operational security practices.

---

## 7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose available information:

- To service providers operating infrastructure
- To comply with legal process
- To investigate abuse or security incidents
- To protect rights, safety, and integrity of users and systems

---

## 8. International Use

The service may be accessed from multiple jurisdictions. You are responsible
for understanding local legal requirements that apply to your use.

---

## 9. Your Choices

You can reduce exposure by:

- Avoiding unnecessary personal data in messages
- Using ephemeral room patterns
- Managing local key material securely
- Ending sessions when finished

Updated versions will be published in this repository and/or service pages.
