# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** March 20, 2026  
**Service:** opsechat  
**Provider:** Hyperion Gray LLC

---

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how opsechat handles information when you use the service.

Opsechat is designed around privacy-preserving communication:
- ephemeral sessions
- in-memory processing
- no intentional message persistence to disk

This policy applies to chat features, email features, and related operational endpoints.

---

## 2. Data We Intentionally Process

Depending on the feature used, opsechat may process:
- session identifiers
- message payloads while in memory
- room IDs and direct message IDs
- timestamps and limited operational metadata
- user-provided email content when using email features

We process this data solely to provide the service.

---

## 3. Data Retention

Opsechat is intentionally ephemeral:
- chat messages expire automatically
- direct messages expire automatically
- stale in-memory state is regularly cleaned

Because data is not designed for long-term retention, content may be unavailable after expiry.

---

## 4. Encryption and Key Handling

- Browser-side encryption features are available for supported chat flows.
- We do not provide guaranteed key recovery.
- You are responsible for safeguarding your keys and credentials.

---

## 5. Cookies and Session State

Opsechat uses session mechanisms required for core functionality such as:
- identity within a chat room
- rate limiting
- request-level continuity

At this time, opsechat is not designed as an advertising platform and does not use ad-tracking cookies.

---

## 6. Security Controls

We use technical and organizational safeguards, including:
- security headers
- request throttling / rate limiting
- input validation and sanitization controls
- least-retention architecture where practical

No security control is perfect; use threat-appropriate precautions when handling sensitive information.

---

## 7. Third-Party and Infrastructure Considerations

Service operation may rely on infrastructure providers and network dependencies. Those providers may process telemetry or logs under their own terms.

Users are responsible for evaluating their own risk posture and local compliance requirements.

---

## 8. Legal Compliance

We may respond to valid legal process and cooperate with lawful investigations.

Given the ephemeral architecture, available historical data may be limited.

---

## 9. User Choices

You may choose not to use the service if this policy does not meet your requirements.

Because opsechat is designed for temporary communications, there may be limited support for retroactive data access requests compared with persistent platforms.

---

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date.

---

For policy questions, open an issue in the project repository or contact the project maintainers through official channels listed in project documentation.
